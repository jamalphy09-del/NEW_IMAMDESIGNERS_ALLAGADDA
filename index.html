Our Story in Allagadda

New Imam Designers opened its first branch near CSI Church in Allagadda on November 12, 2020, with a clear aim: offering fresh, stylish, and comfortable women’s dresses for both everyday wear and special celebrations.

Backed by local shoppers across Allagadda, our journey grew from festive Shararas, silk and Chanderi Anarkalis, Half-Sarees, and party wear to expanding with our second branch in Srinivas Nagar on July 29, 2026.

  • First Branch — November 12, 2020 Started near CSI Church in Allagadda, building a loyal following for trendy ladies’ dresses and daily styles.
  • Second Branch — July 29, 2026 Expanded to Srinivas Nagar / KVSR College Street near Lakshmi Bakery and the RTC Bus Stand to serve even more shoppers.
  • Occasion & Everyday Value Combines festive picks and party models with budget-friendly daily wear, tops, and kurta set combos.
  • Open Every Day Welcoming visitors Monday through Sunday from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM, with updates on YouTube Shorts and Instagram.
